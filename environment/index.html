How an engagement runs

Four stages, each ending in something written you can review before the next begins.

1

Site and soil assessment

Survey, soil testing and water availability, with a written statement of what the site can support.

2

Plan and specification

Species selection, layout, irrigation and the monitoring to be installed.

3

Establishment and build

Planting, infrastructure, fencing and installation of sensing equipment.

4

Management and monitoring

Ongoing maintenance, yield and condition reporting, and adjustment as the site matures.
