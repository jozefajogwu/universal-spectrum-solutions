How an engagement runs

Four stages, each ending in something written you can review before the next begins.

1

Survey and requirements

Site walk, inventory of what exists, and a written statement of what the system has to do.

2

Design and specification

Architecture, bill of materials and a costed proposal you can take to procurement.

3

Build and deploy

Installation, configuration, data migration and testing, with progress reporting throughout.

4

Support and maintain

Defined response times, scheduled maintenance and a named engineer who knows your deployment.
