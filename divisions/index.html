Three ways to work with us

Pick the level of responsibility you want us to carry.

Supply & Install

Equipment delivered and commissioned
  • Specification and sourcing to your requirement
  • Delivery, installation and commissioning
  • Asset register and as-built documentation
  • Handover training for your operators
  • Warranty handling with the manufacturer
Best whenYou already know what you need, and you want it specified, supplied and commissioned properly.

Turnkey Project

Design through to handover
  • Site survey, design and written scope
  • All trades coordinated under one contract
  • Project management and progress reporting
  • Testing, commissioning and full documentation
  • Operator training and 12-month defects support
Best whenThe project crosses several trades and you want one contract, one programme and one name to call.

Managed Programme

We run it after it is built
  • Everything in the turnkey model
  • Scheduled preventive maintenance
  • Defined response times for faults
  • Named engineer who knows your installation
  • Quarterly performance and condition reporting
Best whenThe system matters too much to run unsupported once the installers have left site.
