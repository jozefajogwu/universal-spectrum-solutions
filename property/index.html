How an engagement runs

Four stages, each ending in something written you can review before the next begins.

1

Land and feasibility

Title checks, site survey, and a feasibility study covering cost, demand and services.

2

Masterplan and design

Layout, unit mix, services routing and approvals, with energy and security designed in from the start.

3

Construction and services

Civil works, buildings, roads, drainage, power, security systems and connectivity.

4

Handover and management

Allocation, documentation, and ongoing facility and property management services.
