How an engagement runs

Four stages, each ending in something written you can review before the next begins.

1

Risk and site assessment

Walk the site, identify exposure and agree what the system needs to prevent or record.

2

System specification

Camera positions, coverage maps, door schedules, detection zones and a costed bill of materials.

3

Installation and integration

Containment, cabling, devices and head-end, integrated so one operator can see everything.

4

Testing and scheduled service

Commissioning tests, operator training, and a maintenance schedule with documented inspections.
