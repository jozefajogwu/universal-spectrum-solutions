How an engagement runs

Four stages, each ending in something written you can review before the next begins.

1

Consultancy and strategy

Audience, platform and format decisions before any equipment is specified.

2

Studio and station build

Acoustics, equipment, transmission chain and online infrastructure, installed and tested.

3

Content and production

Programme formats, production schedule and the crew to deliver them.

4

Distribution and management

Transmission, online distribution, archiving, and ongoing station management if you want it run for you.
