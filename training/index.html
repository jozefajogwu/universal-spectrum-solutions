How an engagement runs

Four stages, each ending in something written you can review before the next begins.

1

Scope and audience

Who is being trained, what they need to be able to do afterwards, and how success will be measured.

2

Design and content

Curriculum, materials, facilitators and the schedule, built around the audience rather than an off-the-shelf deck.

3

Delivery and logistics

Venue, equipment, registration, catering and the run of show — on site or at our facilities.

4

Report and follow-up

Attendance, assessment results and a written report, plus follow-up sessions where the programme calls for it.

How learners are assessed. Assessment happens in a working production environment, through direct observation, oral questioning, witness testimony, personal statements or reflective practice, and assignments. Simulation is permitted for health and safety units at the lower levels. Each unit is signed off by the learner and assessor, with internal and external quality assurance sampling.
